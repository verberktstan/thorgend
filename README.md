## Thorgend

An instrument plugin using the dynamic stochastic approach to waveform synthesis conceived by Iannis Xenakis.

The effect can be compiled to VST3, CLAP or AUv2.

## Parameters

### Voices
How many blades the war-party carries. Each voice is a parallel soul singing the same note through its own stochastic fate — stack them and the sound thickens into a chorus of chaos.

### Richness
*Rich: abundant, layered, overflowing with detail.*
The number of waypoints the waveform must traverse each cycle. A sparse few yields gaunt, skeletal tones; a full complement weaves a tapestry so dense it shimmers like dragon-scale armour.

### Noisyness Rate
*Noisome: that which disturbs and unsettles.*
The speed at which the stochastic demon stirs the pot — how fast the pitch and timbre writhe and shift. Low values are slow, ominous dread; high values are the frenzied cackling of a goblin horde mid-ambush.

### Noise Complexity
*Complexity: a labyrinth of interconnected parts, each hiding a new threat.*
Controls how far the stochastic perturbations reach. Turn it low and the waveform breathes quietly; crank it and the sound fractures into wild unpredictability, like a dungeon that keeps reshaping its own corridors.

### Noisyness
The raw bias between order and chaos — a ranger's compass pointing anywhere from perfectly composed to deliriously unhinged.

### Variousity
*Various: manifold, ever-changing, never the same twice.*
The sample-and-hold clock that throws the dice and locks in a new value. At low rates it lurches between frozen states like a troll deciding its next swing; at full speed the changes blur into continuous shimmer, the torchlight never quite settling.

### Dichotomization
*Dichotomy: the cleaving of one thing into two irreconcilable opposites.*
Drives the modulation signal through saturation until it is forced to choose — up or down, light or shadow, silence or fury. Soft settings yield smooth, sinuous curves; high settings snap the waveform into hard, two-faced decisions, like a paladin who has seen too much.

### Attack / Decay / Sustain / Release
The shape of each voice's birth and death: how quickly it rises from silence, how it falls after its peak, the level it holds through the long watch, and how long it takes to die when the key is released.

### Output Gain
The master volume of the keep — how loudly your creation announces itself to the world.

## VST3, CLAP & AUv2 installation

You can download the VST3, CLAP & AUv2 plugins from the [releases page](https://github.com/verberktstan/Thorgend/releases).

On macOS you may need to [disable Gatekeeper](https://disable-gatekeeper.github.io/) as Apple has recently made it more difficult to run unsigned code on macOS.
